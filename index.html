Scoring Intelligence

How The Croupier evaluates candidates.

Unlike generic scrapers that do naive keyword matching, The Croupier's evaluation engine inspects four dimensions before dealing a verdict — and writes out its reasoning so your recruiters can check its work.

Dimension 01
Technical Breadth & Stack Match
Evaluates production depth in required technologies, distinguishing between peripheral exposure and hands-on architecture ownership.
Dimension 02
Career Velocity & Trajectory
Measures promotion cadence, scope expansion across employers, and whether the candidate consistently operates at or above target leveling.
Dimension 03
Systems & Organizational Scale
Calibrates past employer complexity, transaction throughput, and team leadership footprint against your company's stage.
Dimension 04
Flight Risk & Intent Signals
Detects open-to-work flags, tenure stability, and seniority friction to prioritize candidates who are most likely to respond and sign.

Frequently Asked Questions

Answers to common questions.

How does The Croupier differ from LinkedIn Recruiter?
LinkedIn Recruiter is a manual search bar that returns hundreds of unranked profiles for you to read line by line. The Croupier evaluates candidate profiles against your stated criteria automatically, returning a scored shortlist with written Hire / Maybe / Pass verdicts in under 60 seconds.
How does the 3-day trial work?
When you start a subscription, your card is held and verified via Stripe, but it is not charged until Day 4. You get full platform access immediately. If you decide to cancel anytime before Day 4, you pay zero.
Is my candidate search data kept confidential?
Yes. Your role criteria, search history, and talent notes are scoped to your account and never visible to other customers. We don't sell or share your candidate data with third parties.
Which ATS platforms can I export candidates into?
The Croupier integrates natively with 100Hires, Recruitee, JazzHR, SmartSuite, Odoo, Loxo, and Manatal. For anything else, use a custom outbound webhook or export a clean CSV.
